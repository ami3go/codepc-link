CodePC Link

A lightweight Bluetooth Low Energy discovery, network-status, and recovery companion for headless Linux mini-PC systems managed with Cockpit.

Early development · specification / feasibility stage
View on GitHub Roadmap

Discover

Find a nearby CodePC over BLE without knowing its current IP address.

Diagnose

Read Wi-Fi/Ethernet addresses, route state, and selected health information out-of-band.

Open Cockpit

Use BLE to discover the right target, then hand full administration to normal IP networking.
