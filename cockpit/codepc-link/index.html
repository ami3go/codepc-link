Android HID pairing

The phone is a vendor-defined Bluetooth HID device; CodePC is the HID host.

Server-initiated first pairing

Initial pairing starts only from this CodePC. The Android app never creates the bond.

  1. Open the CodePC Link HID app on Android and keep it in the foreground.
  2. Tap Make phone discoverable in the app.
  3. Click Scan for phone here and then Pair from CodePC.
  4. Confirm the Android pairing prompt. After bonding, choose the CodePC host in the app and connect HID.

The HID descriptor uses vendor usage page 0xFF00 and does not declare keyboard or mouse usages.
